## <img src="https://media.giphy.com/media/UVG0BN8TOMKkPOJS6e/giphy.gif" width="28"> &nbsp;My Journey

<details>
<summary><b>🚀 Click to expand the long version</b></summary>




**2022 — Learning the shape of a backend.**
Joined the industry on 15 May 2022. Spent the first stretch getting fluent in Go and Java, writing REST endpoints, learning how a request actually travels through a service, and internalising why "it works locally" is not a milestone.

**2023 — Meeting real traffic.**
Moved onto systems where volume stopped being theoretical. Kafka producers and consumers, Redis for hot state, ScyllaDB and RocksDB for durable high-write paths. Learned that throughput problems and correctness problems look identical at 3 a.m. and must be told apart quickly.

**2024 — Optimization as a job, not a chore.**
Started treating performance as a first-class deliverable. Profiling, allocation reduction, cache design, and the constant negotiation between memory footprint and latency. This is where distributed systems stopped being a buzzword and became a set of trade-offs I could reason about.

**2025 — Owning an ad serving surface.**
Delivery, tracking, attribution, capping — real-time paths where a millisecond and a rupee are the same unit. Tuned Kafka pipelines, cut memory on hot paths, and moved from implementing designs to proposing them.

**2026 — Performance engineering.**
Currently focused on high-TPS systems, cache framework design, and squeezing predictable behaviour out of Go at the edges: GC pressure, goroutine scheduling, lock contention, and the tail of the latency distribution nobody looks at until it's on fire.

</details>
